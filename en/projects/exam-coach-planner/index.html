Exam Coach Planner

Study coach that manages the Turkish university exam journey with weekly plans, score tracking, and progress analytics in one desktop app.

PythonTkinterJSON
GitHub repository← All projects

01Overview

Exam Coach Planner combines weekly study scheduling, mock-exam tracking and progress analytics for students preparing for the Turkish university entrance exams (TYT/AYT). Planning happens by day and hour, from 00:00 to 23:00.

The app supports student profiles: each student has their own weeks, weeks can be copied, and switching between them is instant. The analytics panel on the right shows planned versus completed study, weekly completion rate, total exam net scores and per-subject net trends.

02Key Features

  • Weekly planning — build study blocks on a day/hour grid
  • Student profiles — week management, week copying and quick switching
  • TYT/AYT tracking — per-subject net entry and track-aware AYT total net calculation
  • Analytics panel — planned vs completed bars, completion rate, net charts and subject trends
  • Filters — All, TYT, AYT, last 30 days and last 3 months
  • Quality of life — auto text from course/topic/source, quick suggestions from past entries, one-click auto-plan
  • Data safety — automatic and timestamped backups, JSON import/export and restore

03Engineering Notes

  • All data is stored locally as JSON, and even a full reset takes a backup first.
  • Written in Python with Tkinter, with support for a single-file Windows .exe build via PyInstaller.
