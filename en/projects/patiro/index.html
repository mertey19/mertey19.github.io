Patiro

01Overview

Patiro is a care and tracking app built for people who look after more than one pet. Vaccination dates, feeding routines, vet visits and daily habits live in one place, and every animal gets its own profile, history and reminders.

This project was a turning point for me: not the first app in my portfolio, but the first published one. App Store and Google Play submissions, the privacy policy, store reviews and real user feedback taught me that shipping a product is much more than writing the code.

02Key Features

  • Multi-pet profiles — a separate record, history and care plan per animal
  • Vaccination and vet tracking — upcoming appointments and recurring vaccine schedules
  • Daily care routines — food, water, walk and medication reminders
  • Care history — look back over past records for each pet
  • Live in both stores — iOS and Android releases maintained in parallel

03Engineering Notes

  • The app went through review in both stores; release management, privacy declarations and store assets (screenshots, descriptions) were all mine end to end.
  • Post-launch feedback showed that simplifying the flow matters more than a longer feature list — a priority I have carried into later projects.
